The Philippines is a sprawling tropical archipelago in Southeast Asia featuring thousands of pristine islands, coral reef sanctuaries, diverse marine life, and vibrant cultural traditions. This list is intended for island-hopping travelers, scuba diving enthusiasts, coastal explorers, and tropical adventurers.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, digital customs systems, and weather tracking.

- [Philippine Department of Tourism (DOT)](https://philippinetourismusa.com/) - Official national tourism portal featuring regional highlights, accredited establishments, and cultural guides.
- [eTravel Registration System](https://etravel.gov.ph/) - Official mandatory digital data collection platform required for all arriving and departing passengers (must be completed within 72 hours prior to arrival).
- [PAGASA Weather (Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration)](https://www.pagasa.dost.com.ph/) - Official national meteorological agency tracking tropical cyclones, severe weather advisories, and regional forecasts.

## Transportation & Ferries

Navigating inter-island RORO vessels, high-speed passenger ferries, domestic airlines, and regional transport.

- [2GO Travel](https://www.travel.2go.com.ph/) - Operator of the largest modern RORO (Roll-on/Roll-off) fleet connecting Manila with major island hubs across Visayas and Mindanao.
- [OceanJet Fast Ferries](https://www.oceanjet.net/) - High-speed passenger catamaran service connecting Cebu, Bohol, Siquijor, and Negros Oriental.
- [Philippine Airlines (PAL)](https://www.philippineairlines.com/) - National flag carrier operating extensive domestic connections across the archipelago's regional airports.
- [Cebu Pacific Air](https://www.cebupacificair.com/) - Major domestic low-cost airline providing frequent regional flights across island hubs.

## Destinations & Regions

### Manila & Luzon

- [It's More Fun in the Philippines - Manila Hub](https://philippinetourismusa.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring historical Intramuros, museums, and the capital's modern districts.
- [Banaue Rice Terraces Tourism Portal](https://philippinetourismusa.com/) - Official guide for exploring the 2,000-year-old mountain carved terraces in the Cordillera highlands.
- [Baguio City Tourism Office](https://philippinetourismusa.com/) - Official visitor guide to the "Summer Capital of the Philippines" known for its cool pine climate and creative arts culture.

### Cebu, Bohol & Central Visayas

- [Cebu & Bohol Tourism Portal](https://philippinetourismusa.com/) - Regional travel hub for exploring Chocolate Hills, heritage cathedrals, and pristine central island beaches.

### Palawan (El Nido & Coron)

- [Palawan Tourism Council](https://philippinetourismusa.com/) - Official visitor portal for exploring "the last frontier", limestone karst cliffs, secret lagoons, and sunken World War II wrecks in Coron.

### Boracay & Aklan

- [Boracay Tourism Administration](https://philippinetourismusa.com/) - Official guide for visiting world-famous White Beach, sustainable island regulations, and water sports.

### Siargao & Mindanao Surfing Hubs

- [Siargao Islands Tourism Portal](https://philippinetourismusa.com/) - Official travel hub for Cloud 9 surfing, mangrove lagoons, and island-hopping adventures.

## Diving & Marine Conservation

Resources for discovering world-class coral reefs, marine sanctuaries, and dive centers.

- [Philippine Commission on Sports Scuba Diving (PCSSD)](https://tourism.gov.ph/) - Official government agency regulating scuba diving tourism, safety standards, and dive operator accreditation.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, tropical storm precautions, and travel security.

- [Emergency Hotline (National Emergency Response - Dial 911)](https://www.gov.ph/) - Direct nationwide emergency dispatch number for police, fire, and medical assistance.
- [Philippine National Police Tourist Assistance](https://pnp.gov.ph/) - Dedicated police units deployed across major tourist zones providing traveler safety support.
